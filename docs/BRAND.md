# ICO brand system

**Name:** In Case Of  
**Short mark:** ICO  
**Promise:** Someone notices.  
**Principle:** Monitor the plan, not the person.

In Case Of closes uncertainty; it does not decide whether someone is in danger.

The mark is always the literal letters `ICO`. The orange center of the `O` is the only
signal accent: it represents an unresolved expected moment, not an alarm or a tracked
person. Do not substitute the earlier geometric/radar emblem.

## Core colors

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---:|---|
| Ink | `#171A18` | Primary text and dark field |
| Chalk | `#F6F5F0` | Light text and mark |
| Signal | `#E85B2A` | Unresolved attention and the O center |
| Sea glass | `#9DC9B5` | Confirmed or resolved state |
| Stone | `#555C57` | Rules and secondary structure |

## Files

- `submission/brand/ico-lockup.svg` — primary horizontal identity.
- `submission/brand/ico-mark.svg` — standalone full-size mark.
- `submission/brand/ico-mark-small.svg` — optically simplified mark for small UI.
- `submission/brand/ico-mark-monochrome.svg` — one-color light-background use.
- `submission/brand/ico-mark-inverse.svg` — one-color dark-background use.
- Matching PNG exports are generated from these sources for raster-only upload fields.
- Web favicon/PWA/Apple/OG assets live under `apps/marketing`.
- Android adaptive, round, splash/launcher, and notification assets live under
  `android/app/src/main/res`.

Keep clear space equal to the width of the `I`. Do not recolor the signal dot, add gradients
to the wordmark, or place the mark on busy imagery.
