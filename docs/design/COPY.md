# In Case of — Copy Standards

> **In Case of does not decide whether someone is in danger. It notices unresolved
> expectations and works to close the loop.**

---

## 1. Voice

Concrete, calm, specific. The product speaks like a well-designed instruction card, not like a
brand. Every sentence should survive the question *"is this literally true?"*

---

## 2. Concrete beats abstract

| Never | Use |
|---|---|
| "Revolutionizing personal safety through intelligent autonomous AI." | "Missed your check? In Case of starts with you." |
| "Our advanced AI intelligently orchestrates multi-channel workflows." | "If you still don't respond, it moves to the next person you chose." |
| "Privacy-first." *(alone)* | "Location stays off unless you explicitly add it to a Plan." |
| "AI is thinking..." | "Building your plan…" |
| "What would you like your AI agent to automate?" | "What should happen?" |

Banned words: revolutionize · reimagine · supercharge · unlock · seamless · next-generation ·
future of safety.

Banned content: fake metrics · fake testimonials · fake company logos · fake download numbers.
**We ship zero invented numbers.**

---

## 3. Never speculate about danger

- ✅ "Mona hasn't responded. Expected 9:00 PM. Tried: notification, reminder, call."
- ❌ "Mona may be in danger."

Speculative language appears **only** when it came from an explicit human input. The product states
what happened and what happens next. It does not narrate risk.

---

## 4. Responder messages

A responder may be reading this on a lock screen, at night, with no context. Lead with the person,
then the fact, then the single action.

```
Mona hasn't responded

Evening check · Expected 9:00 PM

In Case of tried:
  9:00  notification
  9:10  reminder
  9:20  phone call

[ I'm checking ]   [ Call Mona ]   Can't respond
```

---

## 5. Confirmations state scope

Ambiguity about *how much* a change affects is a safety problem.

```
Move tonight's check
9:00 PM → 9:30 PM

This only changes tonight.

[ Confirm ]   Cancel
```

Always say what is *not* changing. "This only changes tonight" is the most important line.

---

## 6. Legal-safety answers (verbatim, site + app)

**Is In Case of an emergency service?**
> No. In Case of coordinates the plans and trusted contacts you choose. It is not a substitute for
> local emergency services, medical care or professional monitoring.

**Does it track my location?**
> Not continuously. Location is off by default. Future Plans may optionally allow a limited
> location snapshot under conditions you explicitly approve.

**Does In Case of decide whether I'm in danger?**
> No. A missed Moment creates uncertainty, not a diagnosis. In Case of follows the Plan you
> approved.

This honesty makes the product stronger, not weaker. Do not soften it for marketing.

---

## 7. Positioning

Never pitch "an AI safety app." Pitch:

> **Most safety apps watch people. In Case of watches expectations.**
>
> When something expected doesn't happen, it starts with you, then works through the people you
> chose until the uncertainty is resolved.

Marketing headline: **Someone notices.**

### SEO themes
personal safety check-in · living alone safety app · trusted contact check-in · solo travel
check-in · family check-in app · missed check-in alert

### Never market as
medical alert · emergency response · medical monitoring — unless legal and product scope change.
