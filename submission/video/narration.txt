The security boundary is structural. AWS credentials are the only permitted model path; there is no model-provider key in the website, Lambda code, or APK. AgentCore Gateway tools accept roles and action types, never raw phone numbers, email addresses, or arbitrary URLs. Cedar starts from default deny, and a forbid decision wins. The target Lambda repeats tenant, consent, membership, plan-version, channel, Alert-state, and checking-lease checks immediately before delivery.

Every boundary expects retries and crashes. The workflow persists action intent and progress together. Conditional worker ownership prevents concurrent or replayed queue deliveries from repeating a confirmed provider call. Consent is checked again after enqueue. A provider timeout after possible acceptance becomes an unknown outcome for reconciliation, never a blind resend or a false delivery claim.
